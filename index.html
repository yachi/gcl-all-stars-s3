Round by round from the VODs read off the broadcast

Each round's result and the stats shown on screen when it ended, read from the match videos frame by frame. TETR.IO shows APM, PPS, VS, attack and pieces. Zone Battle rounds also have the in-game result panel: play time, drops per minute, lines attack per minute, Zone attack per minute and rank for each player. Format and prizing

  • 16 All-Stars per title, split into Americas and Europe/Asia groups of 8.
  • Single round robin, 7 weeks: Oct 26 – Dec 13, 2026. Score deadline Sunday 11:59pm ET each week.
  • Zone Battle sets are first to 7 (default Zone Battle settings). TETR.IO sets are first to 15 on the Tetra League preset, with a .ttrm replay required.
  • Disconnects: the remaining player takes a point (max two); a third disconnect forfeits the set. Activity wins are 7–0 / 15–0.
  • Gauntlet Dec 19, 7pm ET: All-Stars 13th–16th vs Open Division Class I top 4, single elimination. Losers drop to Open.
  • Playoffs Jan 2 and Jan 9, 2027, 7pm ET, streamed on twitch.tv/EdgeTE.
